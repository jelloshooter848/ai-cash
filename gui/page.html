Burn policy — what every payment costs

The burn is min(cap, amount × rate ÷ 1,000,000), and it is charged on top of what the recipient gets. A cap of 0 therefore means no burn at all, whatever the rate says — leave both at 0 for a mint where a payment moves the whole amount. For a 1% burn capped at 1,000 mc, set rate 10000 and cap 1000. Amounts at or below the exempt figure never burn. A payment is burned twice: once when the paying wallet splits its coins, and again when the recipient redeems the tokens — both are shown in the cost line before you pay. The mint id and baseline are written into the mint's identity file the first time it runs and cannot be changed afterwards; the burn policy can.
